Paper, contours and ink

The same page, with each contribution shown separately. Look at the edges, the solid ink and the spaces inside the letters. These are material experiments; they do not yet model ageing.

The geometric M, R and rules are drawn from filled outlines in this sketch. Ordinary font text stays fixed. Paper is a procedural hypothesis, not a scan or a calibrated recycled stock.
