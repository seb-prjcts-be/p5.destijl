Draw first, then change the ink

The drawing is all here: a word, a rectangle, ordinary p5 text and a line. Change them in the sketch. The two surface helpers work on the layer you draw.

Start with printAmount = 0 and ageAmount = 0 for clean ink. Try printing at 0.15, then change ageAmount on its own. Each seed chooses a separate pattern; changing an amount keeps that pattern in place.

Run it in your own page

Load p5.js, p5.merz.js, ink.js and surface.js, followed by this sketch. No controls or shared composition file are needed.

The surface helpers are research code. They change pixels on the transparent ink layer; the paper is drawn underneath. Press S to save PNG. This sketch does not export SVG. The ageing effect remains an ink-loss hypothesis.
