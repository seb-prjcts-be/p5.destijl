Letter as plane

Build a word from straight parts. Try different widths, bar thicknesses and colours, and see what remains readable.

A-Z and spaces. Accents are simplified.

What this study explores

An alphabet drawn on a 5 by 5 grid. Letters share a base width. Horizontal and vertical parts make the letters; colour picks out parts of that construction.

The letter shapes, line layout and colour assignments are our interpretation. The screen colours were chosen for this study. They were not measured from an original work. Some letters are unusual; their readability still needs study.

Sources and starting points

Van Doesburg’s alphabet uses equal letter widths and a fixed grid without diagonals, as described by The Foundry Types. The historical alphabet is a reference for further letter studies.

The De Stijl letterhead (1924) and the colour design for Huis De Lange (1917) provide further references. This experiment is our own composition.
