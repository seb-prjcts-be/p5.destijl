These are our own digital compositions, made while studying the variety of materials and relationships in Kurt Schwitters’ work. The helper paint() belongs to this sketch. No new library methods are required. The opening is transparent in a temporary p5.Graphics layer. It is not painted in the background colour.

Load p5.js and the local p5.merz.js before this sketch. Drawing stays in p5; this sketch has no SVG export.
