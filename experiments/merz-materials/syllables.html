These are our own digital compositions, made while studying the variety of materials and relationships in Kurt Schwitters’ work. The helper paint() belongs to this sketch. No new library methods are required. The syllables are selected from Merz 1, page 8, not quotations from the Ursonate. This is a visual study without audio. System fonts may look different on another computer.

Load p5.js and the local p5.merz.js before this sketch. Drawing stays in p5; this sketch has no SVG export.
