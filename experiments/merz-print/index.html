About this study

This is an original page based on the relationships in the Merz and De Stijl examples Seb chose: large letters, bars, different typefaces and space between them. The University of Iowa's Merz archive provides further material for the studies.

Imperfection changes the coverage of the ink layer. The pattern follows positions on the page, so overlapping marks share it. The red plane behind FORM stays clean in this study. Paper ageing and irregular edges are still to be explored.

PNG saves the image you see. This study does not export the ink treatment or system fonts as SVG. Load p5.merz.js and use the Merz API.
