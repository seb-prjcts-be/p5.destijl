Starting point: the different text sizes and open space in Klei (1919). This page is our own composition, with new text, layout and colours.

Page study

Place the heading and small text yourself. Their sizes stay fixed when you change spacing or position.

What SVG saves

The heading uses editable vector paths. Small text remains ordinary text, so its appearance depends on the fonts available. Canvas and SVG clip everything at the page edge. Nothing is automatically scaled to fit.
