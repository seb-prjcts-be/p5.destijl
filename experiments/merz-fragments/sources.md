## Reuse basis

This study selects a title-letter area of the 1923 cover rather than a later
contributor's photograph or text. Iowa identifies Schwitters as the magazine's
editor and the cover's handwritten annotation as his. The title area's individual
design credit has not been separately established from a catalogue record.

The museum records [Schwitters' death in 1948](https://www.guggenheim.org/artwork/artist/kurt-schwitters).
The EU [Copyright Term Directive, Articles 1 and 8](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32006L0116)
sets the ordinary author term at life plus 70 years, counted from the following
1 January. On that basis, his own work is outside that economic copyright term
from 1 January 2019. That does not establish the rights of every contribution
in the magazine or every jurisdiction.

[Iowa's policy](https://www.lib.uiowa.edu/about/policies/cc/) does not require
permission from the Libraries as image custodian. Keep the source credit and
this record with the study. The archival scan is source material and is not
covered by this repository's MIT licence for its code. This note records the
basis used here, not a blanket rights declaration for the collection.
