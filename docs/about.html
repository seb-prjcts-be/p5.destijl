About p5.merz

p5.merz is a small p5.js library for drawing with letters, lines and colour planes. The screen is its first medium. Writing the sketch is part of the work: choosing a word, moving a line, repeating a shape and seeing what changes.

Starting points

Studies of Theo van Doesburg and Kurt Schwitters guide the work: letters as graphic forms, changes of scale, open space, different reading directions and text crossing other shapes. The current alphabet is our own geometric drawing. The compositions are contemporary interpretations.

Drawing in your own sketch

Keep elements in a composition to edit and save them as SVG, or take letter geometry and draw it with ordinary p5 functions. You choose the text, colours and placement. Ordinary p5 text can sit alongside the geometric alphabet.

Work in progress

The first ink study explores imperfect coverage while keeping the composition fixed. Paper ageing is a separate question. The local library now supplies named letter parts alongside whole-text geometry. Surface treatments remain separate studies.

The project was first called p5.destijl. The library now uses p5.merz.js and the Merz API. The DeStijl API alias remains available for existing sketches.
